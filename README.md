A simple tracker for your own bets. It does not take bets or give tips.

## Features

- Log single bets, multis and free bets
- Full and partial cash-outs, and what would have happened if you had kept the bet
- A monthly limit that counts either all stakes or only losses, each with its own amount
- Profit and loss charts by month, sport and bet type
- Bookmaker margin calculator
- Spreadsheet download
- 12 languages

## Your data

There are no accounts. Bets are saved in your own browser with `localStorage`, so nobody else can see them. They do not move between devices, and clearing your browser data deletes them. Use **Settings > Backup** to download a spreadsheet, and restore it on a new device.

## Install it as an app

### Android app

<a href="https://github.com/TWDRunner4/Slipbook/raw/main/Slipbook.apk"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Download%20for%20Android-5B2BC9?style=for-the-badge&logo=android&logoColor=white" alt="Download for Android" height="44"></a>

Tap the button on your Android phone. When the download finishes, open the file and tap **Install**. The first time, Android asks you to allow installs from your browser or Files app.

### Other ways

Open the site link on your phone, then:

- **iPhone:** in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** in Chrome, open the menu and tap **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

It then opens from its own icon, full screen, and works offline.

## Run it

Host the repository with GitHub Pages. Opening `index.html` directly also works, but it can't be installed that way.

Slipbook is for adults (18+). Please bet responsibly. Helplines for France, the UK, the Netherlands and Romania are listed in the app under **Settings**.
